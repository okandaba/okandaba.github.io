Our story

From shoes to growth

Before carpets, before mattresses, before couches, there were shoes. Okandaba started with one young man cleaning sneakers in Zwelethemba. Today it is Okandaba Holdings: a cleaning company working across the Western Cape, and an NPO giving back to the youth.

“Okandaba is community, and the community is Okandaba.”Anga Tyira, founder and director
