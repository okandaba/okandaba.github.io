Uplifting young people in Zwelethemba

Okandaba Youth Development is a non-profit founded in 2025 by Anga Tyira. We give young people the basics they need, and the skills and opportunities to build a future.

Who we are

Every young person has potential

We focus on young people facing hard situations, including homelessness, substance abuse and social isolation. Our mission is to uplift and change their lives by providing essentials like clothing, shoes and food, and by creating chances for personal and professional growth.

We believe in the potential of every young person and want to help them build successful careers. That is why our work goes beyond handouts, into entrepreneurship, networking, business management and marketing.

“Okandaba is community, and the community is Okandaba. There's no one without the other.” Anga Tyira, founder and director

What we do

Clothes for those in need

We collect clothes people no longer wear, clean them and give them to people who need them.

Restored shoes

We collect and restore shoes, so every step is taken with dignity.

Food parcels

We prepare and share food parcels with families in the community.

Skills and careers

Programmes in entrepreneurship, networking, business management and marketing, and help finding jobs and learnerships.

Sport and tournaments

Soccer and netball tournaments between areas, PlayStation tournaments and school tournaments.

Community days

Programmes on Youth Day (16 June), Mandela Day and Heritage Day that bring people together.

Events

What we've done

  1. 16 June 2025

    Okandaba Youth Tournament

    A Youth Day tournament at Zwelethemba Stadium. Teams from areas across Zwelethemba (Mandela, Fletsini, Matyeni, Asla, ATL and Magwaleni) played each other in soccer and netball.

  2. 2026

    Okandaba PlayStation and school tournaments

    The Okandaba PlayStation Tournament 2026, with the previous winner, Qstar "The Wizard", back to defend his title, and a school tournament for Youth Day.

  3. 24 September 2026

    Heritage Day Community Initiative

    At My Spot, Zwelethemba, from 13:00 to 17:00: traditional meals (umphokoqo, pensi, umngqusho and steamed bread) and free snack packs for children, for the children and elderly of Zwelethemba.

Every clean funds the youth
